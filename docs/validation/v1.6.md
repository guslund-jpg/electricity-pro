## Consented hardware-beta mapping (GoodWe or another installation)

No hardware verification recorded. Invite a volunteer to provide only a redacted
mapping; do not request credentials, raw backups or force battery/inverter modes.

- Inverter model/family, battery presence (known/unknown), HA integration and
  versions; no serial number, location, account ID or network address.
- For each proposed source: pseudonym, meaning, unit, device/state class,
  whole-site vs subsystem, AC vs DC, direction/sign, phase aggregation, reporting
  cadence and whether timestamps are receipt or device acquisition time.
- Production must exclude battery discharge. Household load must exclude battery
  charging while including ordinary EV charging. Confirm from documentation or
  naturally observed behaviour; do not infer this from a label such as "load".
- Energy registers must be lifetime counters, not daily-reset totals, and their
  boundary must match the declared channel. Record which sources share a meter.
- Capture consented, near-simultaneous redacted readings during naturally occurring
  import, solar surplus, battery charge/discharge, zero and outages where available.
  Compare counter deltas over aligned intervals and midnight; restart without
  clearing history. Never deliberately corrupt a real reading or reset a meter.
- Record verified mappings, ambiguous/rejected mappings and unsupported features.
  A working HA GoodWe integration alone is not verified Electricity Pro compatibility.
