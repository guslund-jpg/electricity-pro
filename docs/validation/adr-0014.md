  **Europe/Stockholm**. Times below are local CET (UTC+01:00). Other runtime
  component/Python versions were not separately recorded for this run.
- Installation: the maintainer reported completing a named pre-upgrade HA
  backup. Original integration code, the integration's configuration entry,
  entity/device registry records and statistics were also saved privately.
  The candidate's 47 tracked component files were copied over Samba and verified

  SE3/SEK; VAT-exclusive spot price plus configured VAT/charges; local supplier
  cost estimation; fixed Good Time threshold; seasonal high/low grid tariff
  with Workday. Optional production/export/household channels and their
  compatibility diagnostic are off.

manual coverage of all 50 possible entities, other source configurations or
