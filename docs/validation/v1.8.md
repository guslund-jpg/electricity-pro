## Versioned package smoke test — 21 November 2026

The maintainer approved a new recovery backup, installation of the candidate
from PR #431, integration reload and HA restart, while explicitly withholding
merge and publication. A fresh full HA backup included Recorder; it and a
targeted integration/settings/dashboard archive were copied off the instance
and their SHA-256 checksums verified before installation.

All **55** installed files matched candidate **4490d8c** and the test ZIP
fingerprint below. `ha core check` passed, then HA restarted successfully to load
**1.8.0b1**. The integration was reloaded and HA restarted once more. The source
installation was the already-tested development build with a 1.7.2 manifest,
not a fresh installation of published v1.7.2. This is manual package/lifecycle
evidence; no new Marketplace/HACS upgrade from published v1.7.2 is claimed.

Before data was captured at **10:00:22 CET**. Each settled reading was compared
with that same baseline:

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| After upgrade restart | 10:10:26 | 0.703 kWh | 0.703 kWh each | Passed |
| After integration reload | 10:10:48 | 0.729 kWh | 0.729 kWh each | Passed |
| After final HA restart | 10:17:12 | 0.810 kWh | 0.810 kWh each | Passed |

The comparisons verified:

- All **52 existing registry identities**, names, device associations and
  disabled choices were preserved. No entities disappeared or were added.
  Settings/options, configuration YAML, all saved dashboards, the MQTT mirror
  and its Recorder exclusion matched their before fingerprints.
- All previously available values and public attribute keys returned after
  ordinary one-/five-minute publications. Remaining Cost and the weighted
  average were initially unavailable after core startup and recovered without
  forced entity updates. The settled checks do not imply uninterrupted startup
  availability.
- Fingerprints of **40,104 hourly** and **75,499 five-minute** historical
  statistics rows in the stable pre-test prefix were unchanged. Recent buckets
  could continue aggregating. Existing statistics metadata, checked completed
  summaries and checkpoint field keys were retained.
- Both compensation amounts and their attributes remained **0 SEK**, with
  unchanged history starts and partial zero-energy coverage. All four saved
  price periods, including the single active period, were retained exactly.
  At the final check, **52 hourly** and **622 five-minute** compensation rows
  had state **0** and sum **0**. The fresh MQTT mirror still had **zero**
  Recorder state rows.
- The forecast retained **96 SE3/SEK intervals** and its metadata/boundaries.
  All 96 price values changed slightly when fresh data was fetched at startup.
  A separate native Nord Pool action confirmed every updated price exactly
  after MWh-to-kWh normalization. Reload and final restart retained that same
  post-upgrade response. This is verified source-data refresh, not a claim that
  the forecast matched the pre-upgrade response byte for byte.
- Current Cost Rate matched power / 1000 × Effective Price exactly. Saved
  dashboard configurations stayed unchanged and all eight export coverage
  notes rendered with the known-zero values after each lifecycle step. No new
  visual screenshot review of the versioned package is implied.
- The captured integration/template log windows had no new relevant warnings
  or errors, excluding HA's ordinary custom-integration startup notice.

No real nonzero export, signed live compensation, new calendar boundary or
supplier settlement was tested in this run. Those open limits remain in #241/#335.
