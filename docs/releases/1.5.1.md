# Electricity Pro 1.5.1 — Metering and Cost Reliability

A maintenance release focused on reliable consumption tracking, clearer costs
and easier configuration before the v1.6 production/import/export work.

## Highlights

- Protect lifetime energy and local costs against temporary backward meter
  readings. Recovery no longer counts as new consumption. Protection survives
  outages, midnight and restarts; genuine meter resets require confirmation.
- Estimate supplier costs from observed energy and prices when the meter or
  supplier does not provide daily cost totals. No additional helper is needed.
- Add daily and monthly household cost estimates combining effective variable
  costs with accrued fixed supplier and grid fees. Keep supplier-only totals
  separate and show partial coverage clearly in both example dashboards.
- Correct VAT handling for live and forecast prices using an explicit rate,
  and show a separate VAT-inclusive supplier-price series in the price charts.
- Scope monthly energy tracking to its source and provide an explicit monthly
  energy reset action for incorrect totals.
- Exclude Electricity Pro outputs from source selectors, simplify Tibber
  forecast settings, and group Good Time method with its fixed threshold.

## Before upgrading

Take a Home Assistant backup. Install the update and restart Home Assistant.
An upgrade preserves existing totals: it does not automatically correct earlier
overcounting or rewrite Recorder history. A restored backup can contain older
code and older statistics.

### Review pricing settings

If using native Nord Pool forecasts or a VAT-excluded live price source, enter
the applicable VAT percentage in Electricity Pro settings. No rate is inferred
from currency or location; use explicit zero when appropriate. Until configured,
VAT-dependent calculations can be unavailable while raw market charts remain
visible. Supplier markup and tariff inputs are VAT-inclusive.

For Tibber fast track, Nord Pool forecast VAT and markup are in a separate
forecast-pricing step when forecasting is selected. Tibber live prices are not
taxed or marked up again. See [VAT pricing](../vat-pricing.md).

### Review energy and cost sources

For P1IB or another lifetime import meter, select Total accumulated energy.
Leave Today's accumulated cost sensor empty if no external daily total exists;
a fixed monthly fee is not a daily cost source. Local cost estimation requires
compatible prices and observed energy updates.

An existing external supplier cost sensor remains authoritative for supplier
totals. Household cost estimates are calculated separately to avoid adding the
same charges twice. See [local costs](../local-cost-estimates.md) and
[household costs and fixed fees](../household-cost-estimates.md).

### Update copied dashboards

Refresh manually copied example YAML to display the new supplier-price series,
household totals and compact coverage notes. Existing copied YAML does not
change just because the integration is upgraded. New sensor history starts
when those sensors are installed; it is not backfilled.

### Do not reset a meter to repair a total

Confirm meter reset is only for a verified physical meter replacement/reset.
Do not use it for temporary drops or outages. Reset monthly energy affects only
the monthly energy total, not daily energy, costs or historical charts. Review
[meter protection](../meter-reading-protection.md) and
[monthly recovery](../monthly-energy-recovery.md) before using either action.

## Coverage and limitations

These costs are estimates, not guaranteed bill totals. Unobserved or uncertain
pricing intervals are excluded, and energy and cost can cover different periods.
Fixed fees accrue from calendar midnight/month start even when variable-cost
tracking starts later. Demand charges and other unconfigured charges are excluded.
Monthly peak-hour derivation and historical cost reconstruction remain follow-ups.

## Validation

- Automated coverage includes backward readings, recovery, persistence,
  day/month boundaries, missing data and explicit reset confirmation.
- A latest-version P1IB 15-minute check showed matching 0.33 kWh increases in
  the raw meter, daily energy and monthly energy.
- The following morning, the raw meter increased 15.27 kWh while monthly energy
  increased 15.28 kWh, consistent within display rounding/capture timing. Daily
  totals were consistent with rollover, with 11.54 kWh consumed and 11.52 kWh priced.
- A naturally occurring dip after the fix has not been observed live; that
  scenario is covered by automated regression tests, not claimed as field-tested.

Use the [installation verification checklist](../installation-verification.md)
after upgrading, especially when restoring to another Home Assistant host.
